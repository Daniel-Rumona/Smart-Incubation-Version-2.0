Interventions
Build the plan in the app, sign it on the paper. A draft opens on Build: the SWOT as four short lists, the plan's interventions (✕ to remove, add from the catalogue) and the AI suggestions beside them. Document is the read-only paper exactly as it will be signed and downloaded. "Sign and confirm" always switches to the Document first, so what you sign is what you've just seen. A confirmed plan opens straight on its Document with live status and signatures. The four cards go: the status switch shows the counts. On phones the queue is the page and a plan opens full screen with a Back button.
