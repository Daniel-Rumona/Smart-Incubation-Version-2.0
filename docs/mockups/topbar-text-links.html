Page
Text links that stay text. Page names sit on the left as plain links and are the last thing to give way. The current page is accent text with a thin underline instead of a filled pill. Pages that aren't shortcuts live in More ▾, a menu with a line of description each, instead of a separate Menu button. Click More, and on the funder the scope picker, to open them.
Home is the Agentic space. You land on Home, the assistant with today's key figures, and it's underlined, so you always know where you are. The dashboard is the next link along, one click away. Moving between the assistant and any page is just clicking a link: no Agentic / Workspace switch and no extra Assistant button. Switch to "Without Agentic": the same bar, with Home as the dashboard and no Dashboard link.
Your things on the right. The scope picker (one control, even for a funder organisation), notifications and your profile. The H tile also goes Home.
